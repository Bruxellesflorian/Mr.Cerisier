# Version 0.3 — bâtiments distincts et intérieurs

Images créées avec l’outil intégré image_gen. L’extérieur précédent a été utilisé comme cible d’édition, en conservant portes et chemins. L’atlas est un nouvel asset de six cases ; les cinq premières servent aux cinq bâtiments du jeu.

- `rue-identites.png` : bibliothèque gothique, pub à colombages, brasserie industrielle, serre botanique et club Art déco.
- `interieurs-atlas.png` : bibliothèque et bars, lus par `interiors.js` sans découper le fichier original.
- `atmosphere.js` : fumée, braises, flammes et lueur calculées en temps réel. Les effets respectent la préférence système de réduction des animations.

## Prompt extérieur

Edit this game street background. Preserve EXACT layout, scale, canvas proportions, all five door locations and every cobblestone walkway. Change ONLY architectural appearance of the five main buildings so each silhouette and facade is unmistakably different, while retaining rich detailed nocturnal pixel art. Top-left library: pale limestone gothic academy, tall blue slate mansard roof, stained glass book windows and ivy, one square stone chimney. Upper-right first pub: rustic timber framed English pub, cream plaster, green shutters, asymmetrical red tiled roof, brick chimney. Middle-left brewery: industrial red brick workshop with flat roof, tall copper chimney and large copper vats visible, no triangular gable. Middle-right third bar: overgrown conservatory greenhouse, curved glass roof and wrought iron, trailing hops, warm green glow with restrained pink BAR sign, a slim stove chimney. Lower-left final bar: Art Deco jazz lounge, flat stepped black roof, burgundy facade, vertical violet neon, gold geometric doors, small metal chimney. Keep door bottoms at exactly same positions, keep surrounding street and secondary buildings unchanged. No people. No UI. No baked smoke (will be animated in engine). Rich variation in shapes and materials, not just recoloring. Preserve all paths.

## Prompt atlas intérieur

Each tile is a complete square front-facing slightly elevated RPG interior. Consistent rich detailed 32-bit pixel art with dark timber, atmospheric amber lamps, tactile textures, no characters, NO text or labels. ALL SIX tiles: top 55% furnished back wall, lower 45% mostly clear walkable wooden or stone floor; clear central aisle down to bottom edge; furniture only at sides of lower floor. Small stone fireplace on LEFT rear side in every tile, at local x=15%, y=50%, with BLACK EMPTY FIREBOX (fire added by game animation). Central interactive book lectern or bar counter ends at local y=58%, accessible from below. Row1 col1: gothic library with soaring bookshelves, blue stained glass, central book lectern, oriental carpet. Row1 col2: rustic English pub, oak beams, green upholstery, bottles and central counter. Row1 col3: industrial copper brewery, brick walls, copper tanks and pipework, tasting counter. Row2 col1: botanical greenhouse bar, curved glass panels, lush hanging plants, teal floor tiles, wooden counter. Row2 col2: luxurious Art Deco jazz lounge, burgundy velvet at sides, violet neon, geometric gold details, piano on right, central counter. Row2 col3: quiet wine cellar storage room. CRITICAL: exactly 3 by 2 aligned equal square tiles, each room a standalone background. No characters, no sprites, no interface, no labels.
